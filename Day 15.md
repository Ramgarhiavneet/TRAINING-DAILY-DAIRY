DAY 15 – AI Agent Development

Date:10/7/2026

Today, I worked on developing the AI agent functionality for my project, **Veltrix AI – Gemini**. The main objective was to make the application capable of understanding 
instructions written in natural language so that users could create reminders without manually entering every detail.

With the guidance of my project mentor, I designed the basic workflow of the AI agent. I focused on how user instructions would be received by the application, processed 
through the Google Gemini API, and converted into meaningful information that could be used by other modules.

I also prepared prompts for Gemini to guide the AI model in understanding reminder-related commands. I explored how the model could identify important details such as task 
descriptions, contact names, dates, and times from user instructions.

Furthermore, I worked on the concept of structured AI responses so that the application could process the generated information more easily. I tested different user commands
to observe how Gemini responded to various instructions and identify areas where prompt improvements were needed.

**Work Performed:**
- Designed the basic workflow of the AI agent.
- Prepared and tested prompts for the Google Gemini API.
- Worked on processing natural-language user instructions.
- Explored structured AI responses for extracting reminder details.
- Tested different reminder-related user commands.
- Observed AI responses and identified areas for improvement.
- Discussed the AI agent workflow with my project mentor.

**Learning Outcome:**

Through this activity, I learned how to design an AI agent that processes natural-language instructions using a generative AI model. I understood the importance of prompt 
engineering and structured responses in making AI outputs useful for an application. I also gained practical knowledge of connecting user instructions with application 
logic to simplify the reminder creation process.
