DAY 24 – AI-Based Call Content

Date: 22/7/2026

Today, I focused on using Google Gemini to generate meaningful and natural-sounding reminder messages for the Veltrix AI project. The main objective was to convert saved task 
information into clear messages that could be delivered during automated phone calls. With guidance from my mentor, I explored prompt preparation and tested how Gemini could 
generate personalized messages according to different reminder details.

**Work Performed:**
- Prepared prompts for generating reminder messages using Gemini.
- Generated sample call scripts for different reminder situations.
- Worked on dynamic message generation based on saved task information.
- Explored personalized messages using contact names and task details.
- Tested different reminder scenarios to check the quality of AI-generated content.
- Reviewed the generated messages for clarity and suitability for voice communication.
- Discussed prompt improvements and message structure with my mentor.

**Learning Outcome:**

I learned how Gemini can convert structured task information into natural-language reminder messages. I also understood the importance of prompt design and personalization in 
making automated calls more meaningful and user-friendly.
