DAY 23 – Voice Call Workflow

Date: 21/7/2026

Today, I worked on designing the workflow that connects the voice generation and automated calling components of the Veltrix AI project. The main objective was to convert 
reminder information into spoken content and prepare it for delivery through a phone call. With guidance from my mentor, I explored how ElevenLabs and Twilio could communicate
with each other to support the automated calling process.

**Work Performed:**
- Designed the basic workflow for automated reminder calls.
- Worked on generating voice content from reminder information.
- Explored the integration of ElevenLabs-generated audio with Twilio.
- Connected saved reminder details with the proposed call message content.
- Reviewed API communication between the voice generation and calling services.
- Studied the sequence of processing reminder information, generating speech, and initiating a call.
- Discussed the workflow and integration approach with my mentor.

**Learning Outcome:**

I understood how different APIs can work together to create an automated voice-calling system. I also learned how reminder information can be converted into speech and 
prepared for delivery through a telephony service.
