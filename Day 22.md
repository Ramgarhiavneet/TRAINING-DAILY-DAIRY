DAY 22 – ElevenLabs Integration

Date:20/7/2026

Today, I explored ElevenLabs as the text-to-speech service for the Veltrix AI project. The main objective was to generate natural-sounding AI voices that could be used during 
automated reminder calls. With guidance from my mentor, I studied the ElevenLabs API, explored its voice generation features, and understood how text can be converted into 
spoken audio.

**Work Performed:**
- Explored the ElevenLabs platform and its API documentation.
- Configured access to the ElevenLabs voice generation service.
- Studied the process of converting text into speech.
- Tested the basic text-to-speech functionality.
- Explored different voice settings and available voice options.
- Studied how generated audio could be used in automated phone calls.
- Planned the integration of ElevenLabs with the Twilio calling module.

**Learning Outcome:**

I learned how ElevenLabs can convert written text into natural-sounding speech. I also understood the importance of voice settings and how text-to-speech technology can make
automated reminder calls more interactive and user-friendly.
