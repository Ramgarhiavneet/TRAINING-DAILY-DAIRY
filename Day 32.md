DAY 32 – User Interface Refinement

Date: 3/8/2026

- Refined the sidebar information and service status indicators.

- Checked spacing, alignment, and readability of interface elements.
- Reviewed the overall user experience and identified areas for further improvement.

**Learning Outcome:**
